# **Global Analysis of Universities' Success**  

## **Project Overview**  
This project conducts a **comprehensive global analysis of university success** by integrating data from multiple ranking systems and evaluating key performance metrics. The analysis leverages **Power BI, Excel, and SQL** to process, visualize, and derive insights from structured datasets.  

The study aims to:  
- Compare university rankings across different systems.  
- Evaluate the influence of ranking criteria on university performance.  
- Analyze dynamic shifts in university metrics over time.  
- Identify regional trends and correlations between different success factors.  

---

## **Project Significance**  
Understanding university rankings and performance metrics is crucial for:  
- **Students & Parents:** Making informed decisions about higher education.  
- **Universities & Administrators:** Identifying strengths and areas for improvement.  
- **Policy Makers & Researchers:** Benchmarking global education standards.  

By applying a **structured MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) approach**, this project ensures a systematic breakdown of data, leading to meaningful insights.

## **Dataset Description**  
The dataset includes structured tables with key information on universities, rankings, and performance metrics:  

### **Key Tables:**  
1. **Country** – Unique IDs and names of countries.  
2. **University** – University IDs, country associations, and names.  
3. **Ranking System** – Different ranking methodologies (e.g., QS, THE).  
4. **Ranking Criteria** – Metrics used in rankings (e.g., research output, student ratio).  
5. **University Year** – Annual student metrics (enrollment, international %).  
6. **University Ranking Year** – Yearly ranking scores per university.  

---

## **Project Objectives**  
1. **Evaluate & Compare University Rankings** – Across different systems.  
2. **Identify Key Ranking Factors** – What criteria most influence rankings?  
3. **Conduct Regional Analysis** – How do universities perform by country?  
4. **Explore Long-Term Trends** – How have rankings changed over time?  
5. **Perform Correlation Analysis** – Relationships between different metrics.  
6. **Create Data Visualizations** – Interactive dashboards for insights.  
7. **Implement Predictive Modeling** – (Future Scope) Forecasting trends.  

---

## **Key Insights from EDA & Power BI**  
- **Regional Patterns:** Certain countries dominate top rankings.  
- **Impact of Criteria:** Research output and faculty-student ratio heavily influence rankings.  
- **Temporal Changes:** Some universities show consistent improvement, while others decline.  
- **Correlation Analysis:** Strong links between funding, research, and rankings.
